# Email templates

## Follow-up
```
Subject: Following up on <topic>

Dear <Name>,

I hope you are doing well. I just wanted to kindly follow up on <topic>.
Whenever you have a moment, I would really appreciate an update.

Thank you very much for your time.

Best,
Murray
```

## Thank-you
```
Subject: Thank you

Dear <Name>,

Thank you so much for <what they did>. It truly helped me with <result>,
and I am very grateful for your support.

Best,
Murray
```
